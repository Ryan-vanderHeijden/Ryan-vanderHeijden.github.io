The conterminous U.S. is divided into hexagons, and each hexagon draws the loops of the streamgages inside it as one bundle. Each loop is one gage's average year of daily flow, starting January 1 at the top and running clockwise; distance from the center is flow relative to that gage's mean. Color shows when most of each river's water arrives.

Snowmelt rivers line the Rockies, the northern plains peak in April, the East peaks in late winter, and Florida and the Southwest monsoon peak in summer. The faint texture underneath is 2.6 million NHDPlus catchments: fine fabric where drainage is dense, dark where it is sparse.
