Each row is one streamgage and time runs left to right from 1979 to 2023. Each streak is one drought, colored by how long it lasted. Sorting the rows west to east keeps regions together, so the long multi-year western droughts (California in 1987–92 and 2012–16, the West in 2020–22) sit at the top and the shorter, sharper eastern ones sit below.

The strip along the top is the share of gages in drought on each day, which peaks at 29% in early 1981. The strip on the right is each gage's mean drought length.
