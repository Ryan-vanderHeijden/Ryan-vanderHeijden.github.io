Baseflow is the part of streamflow fed by groundwater. Here it is separated from each gage's daily record with a three-pass Lyne–Hollick filter, then averaged by day of year. The loops are smoother and rounder than the total-flow loops because storm peaks are filtered out. Color shows when each river's baseflow peaks.
