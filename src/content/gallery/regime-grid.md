Each bundle holds the rivers of one HUC02 water-resource region. Each loop is one streamgage's average year of daily flow, starting January 1 at the top and running clockwise, and color shows when each river's water arrives.

The regions have recognizable shapes. The Souris-Red-Rainy basin forms a heart from its sharp April snowmelt pulse, Texas is spiky and flashy, and the South Atlantic-Gulf region splits in two, with Florida's late-summer peak pulling away from the rest of the Southeast.
