Each trail is one rain system tracked day by day through gridMET's 4 km daily rainfall, brightening toward its last day. Color is the season of its wettest day, and brightness is the volume of rain. Winter atmospheric rivers hitting the West Coast separate clearly from summer systems across the interior.

The tracks come from linking daily rain cells of 25 mm or more into paths, then keeping systems that lasted 3–6 days, dropped at least 150 mm somewhere, and traveled 10° or more. Daily data only allows approximate tracking, so these are plausible system paths, not verified storm tracks.
