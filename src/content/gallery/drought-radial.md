Each ring is one streamgage, ranked from west (inside) to east (outside). Time runs clockwise from 1979, just right of the top, to 2023, just left of it. Each arc is one drought: at least 30 days with daily flow below the 10th percentile for that day of the year. Color shows how long the drought lasted, from dim purple for a month to cream for most of a year.

National droughts become spokes that cut across every ring (1980–81, 1988, 2002, 2012). Regional droughts stay confined to part of the radius.
